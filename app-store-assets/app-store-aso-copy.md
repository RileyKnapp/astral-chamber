## Why No Music?

Most meditation and binaural apps bury the actual binaural beat under music, pads, melodies, and layered sound effects. That can sound pretty, but it can also interfere with the binaural experience.

Astral Chamber keeps the experience clean and intentional.

Binaural beats work by sending slightly different frequencies to each ear. Your brain perceives the difference between them as a rhythmic pulse, which may help guide your mind toward states associated with relaxation, meditation, focus, theta, delta, alpha, and other brainwave ranges.

When music is layered too heavily over binaural tones, it can mask the effect, pull your attention outward, or disrupt the subtle frequency relationship between the left and right ear.

Astral Chamber is built for people who want to go inward.
